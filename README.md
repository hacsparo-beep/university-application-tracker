University Application Tracker

Students applying to universities have to manage many deadlines,
documents, and requirements at once, and it's easy to miss something.
This command-line tool helps students (and the people who help them)
see which applications are in progress, which documents are still
missing, and which deadlines are coming up next.

Users: high school students applying to university, and
advisors or tutors who support them.

Interface track: Command-line menu.
